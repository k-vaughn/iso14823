# 41-194 · Cyclists riding in the opposite direction

<p class="sign-kicker"><a href="../categories/41/">Supplementary panel</a> · pictogram code 41-194 · five-digit code 41194</p>
<p class="sign-chips"><span class="chip chip-current">Current</span> <a class="chip chip-draft" href="../#draft-watermark">Draft</a></p>
<p>The sign, if placed under a &quot;One-way traffic&quot; sign, informs drivers that cyclists are allowed to ride in the opposite direction. The driver must adapt his behaviour to this fact, in particular his obligation not to endanger other road users (cyclists).</p>
