# 41-175 · Except for local traffic vehicles

<p class="sign-kicker"><a href="../categories/41/">Supplementary panel</a> · pictogram code 41-175 · five-digit code 41175</p>
<p class="sign-chips"><span class="chip chip-current">Current</span> <a class="chip chip-draft" href="../#draft-watermark">Draft</a></p>
<p>Indicates that the main sign is applicable to all vehicles except for vehicles of residents and or visitors of the area temporarily terminating their travel at a point within the affected area further down the road.</p>
