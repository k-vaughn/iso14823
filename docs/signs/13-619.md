# 13-619 · Slow vehicle lane

<p class="sign-kicker"><a href="../categories/13/">Informative</a> · pictogram code 13-619 · five-digit code 13619</p>
<p class="sign-chips"><span class="chip chip-current">Current</span> <a class="chip chip-draft" href="../#draft-watermark">Draft</a></p>
<p>The sign indicates the number and arrangement of lanes. The sign also indicates an additional lane on the motorway and motor vehicle road for motor vehicles with a slower speed according to national regulations.</p>
