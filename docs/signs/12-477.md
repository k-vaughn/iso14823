# 12-477 · No entry for vehicles carrying more than a certain quantity of substances liable to cause water pollution

<p class="sign-kicker"><a href="../categories/12/">Regulatory</a> · pictogram code 12-477 · five-digit code 12477</p>
<p class="sign-chips"><span class="chip chip-current">Current</span> <a class="chip chip-draft" href="../#draft-watermark">Draft</a></p>
<p>Notice that vehicles carrying more than a certain quantity of substances liable to cause water pollution are forbidden to pass through the designated section of the road.</p>
