# 41-192 · No time charge and no toll

<p class="sign-kicker"><a href="../categories/41/">Supplementary panel</a> · pictogram code 41-192 · five-digit code 41192</p>
<p class="sign-chips"><span class="chip chip-current">Current</span> <a class="chip chip-draft" href="../#draft-watermark">Draft</a></p>
<p>Indicates a section of motorway that is not subject to time or toll charges. The sign may also be used to indicate a section of the road which, unlike the preceding section, is not subject to a time or toll charge.</p>
