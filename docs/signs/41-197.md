# 41-197 · Transit

<p class="sign-kicker"><a href="../categories/41/">Supplementary panel</a> · pictogram code 41-197 · five-digit code 41197</p>
<p class="sign-chips"><span class="chip chip-current">Current</span> <a class="chip chip-draft" href="../#draft-watermark">Draft</a></p>
<p>The sign restricts the validity of the marking to goods vehicles for which the use of the section so marked is not necessary to reach the place of loading, unloading, maintenance or repair of the vehicle, the registered office, place of business or usual residence of the haulier or driver.</p>
