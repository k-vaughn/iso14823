# 11-269 · Uneven road (hump bridge)

<p class="sign-kicker"><a href="../categories/11/">Warning</a> · pictogram code 11-269 · five-digit code 11269</p>
<p class="sign-chips"><span class="chip chip-current">Current</span> <a class="chip chip-draft" href="../#draft-watermark">Draft</a></p>
<p>Warning of a road humps or series of road humps on road surface ahead that may hinder travelling at normal speeds.</p>
